A little about me

Design rooted in detail.

I'm Zainab Shahnawaz, a creative textile designer specializing in print development and surface design. My passion lies in creating distinctive prints, patterns, and designs for a variety of surfaces through different printing techniques.

I enjoy exploring creative concepts and transforming them into unique designs using both manual and digital approaches. With an understanding of patterns, colours, textures, and surface exploration, I aim to develop innovative and visually engaging designs that combine creativity with functionality.

My Journey

Experience & Expertise

Fashion & Apparel

Assistant Designer

Ikhbaat

My experience at Ikhbaat has expanded my expertise from Textile Design into Fashion and Apparel Design. I have gained hands-on experience in garment design, collection development, and production processes, working from initial concepts to finished garments.

My role combines creative design, technical development, and management responsibilities.

Key Experience

  • Fashion Illustration & Croqui Painting
  • Surface Design & Embellishment Planning
  • Motif Development & Embroidery Layouts
  • Capsule Collection & Styling Concepts
  • Fashion Research & Mood Boards
  • Production Coordination & Design Revisions
  • Documentation, Size Charts & Costing
